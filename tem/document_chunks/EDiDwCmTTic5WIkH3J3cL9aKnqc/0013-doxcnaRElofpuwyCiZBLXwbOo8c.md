# 管理员手册

## Workspace administrators

A workspace administrator is the administrator of a single workspace. These administrators have management permissions for a specific workspace, where they can change name, cover, manage members and set advanced permissions. 

By default, the creator of a workspace is its administrator. The creator can assign other users as workspace administrators in the workspace settings page.
